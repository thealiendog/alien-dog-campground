RENTAL AGREEMENT, WAIVER AND CANCELLATION POLICY

Bookings made on aliendogcampground.com are made with Evolved Acquisitions LLC, which operates Alien Dog Campground in Joshua Tree, California.

CANCELLATION POLICY

Bookings of fewer than 28 nights follow our Limited policy. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

  • 14 or more days before check-in: full refund.
  • 7 to 14 days before check-in: 50% of the nightly total refunded, plus taxes on the refunded amount.
  • Less than 7 days before check-in: the nightly total is not refunded.
  • Cleaning fee: refunded whenever the nights are refunded, and not refunded when they are not.
  • 24-hour grace period: cancel within 24 hours of booking for a full refund, as long as check-in is at least 14 days away.
  • Stays of 28 nights or more: full refund up to 30 days before check-in. After that, the first 30 days of the stay are not refundable.

To cancel, email onlinesupport@thealiendog.com from the address you booked with.
