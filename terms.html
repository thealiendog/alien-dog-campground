RENTAL AGREEMENT, WAIVER AND CANCELLATION POLICY

Bookings made on aliendogcampground.com are made with Evolved Acquisitions LLC, which operates Alien Dog Campground in Joshua Tree, California.

CANCELLATION POLICY

Bookings of fewer than 28 nights follow our Limited policy. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

  • 14 or more days before check-in: full refund.
  • 7 to 14 days before check-in: 50% of the nightly total refunded, plus taxes on the refunded amount.
  • Less than 7 days before check-in: the nightly total is not refunded.
  • Cleaning fee: refunded whenever the nights are refunded, and not refunded when they are not.
  • 24-hour grace period: cancel within 24 hours of booking for a full refund, as long as check-in is at least 14 days away.
  • Stays of 28 nights or more: full refund up to 30 days before check-in. After that, the first 30 days of the stay are not refundable.

To cancel, email onlinesupport@thealiendog.com from the address you booked with.

RENTAL AGREEMENT AND WAIVER: HOUSE BOOKING

This Rental Agreement (“Agreement”) is between Evolved Acquisitions LLC, which operates Alien Dog Campground in Joshua Tree, California (“Owner”), and the individual completing the direct booking (“Renter,” and together with all other occupants, campers, and visitors during the reservation, “Guests”). The rented premises are the Alien Dog Campground property in Joshua Tree, California (the “Property”).

This Agreement applies to rentals of the house and the surrounding 5-acre property for up to four (4) Guests.

By checking the acceptance box during online booking and completing payment, Renter agrees to be bound by this Agreement on behalf of Renter and all Guests, including any minors accompanying Renter.

1. Term

This Agreement begins on the arrival date (“Check-In”) and ends on the departure date (“Check-Out”) in the confirmed booking, for the number of nights stated there. The Property will be ready for occupancy no earlier than 4:00 PM Pacific Time on the Check-In date and must be fully vacated, with all Guests and belongings removed, no later than 10:00 AM Pacific Time on the Check-Out date, unless Owner agrees otherwise in writing.

2. Rent

The total amount due, including the nightly rate, cleaning fee, any pet fee, and applicable Transient Occupancy Tax, is shown at checkout and is payable in full at the time of booking.

3. Limited Occupancy

Occupancy is strictly limited to the number of Guests in the confirmed booking, not to exceed four (4) persons. If Owner determines this limit has been exceeded, or that unregistered guests are present, Owner may immediately remove the entire party from the Property with no refund of any amount paid.

4. Non-Disturbance and Conduct

Renter and all Guests agree not to disturb, endanger, or create a nuisance for neighbors or nearby properties. Quiet hours are 11:00 PM to 7:00 AM: outside noise, including music and amplified sound, must be kept to a minimum during those hours. Fireworks, and open flames outside the designated fire pit area, are prohibited. Renter and all Guests agree to observe all posted parking restrictions and to park only in designated areas. Firearms and other weapons are not permitted on the Property.

5. Fire Pit and Outdoor Areas

Renter is responsible for ensuring all Guests use only the designated fire pit for fires and fully extinguish any fire before leaving it unattended or going to sleep. Food, trash, and scented items must be securely stored to avoid attracting desert wildlife.

6. Pets and Dogs

Dogs only. Up to two (2) dogs are permitted on this booking, subject to the pet fee charged at checkout. Animals not disclosed at booking may result in additional charges or removal. Renter is solely responsible for the behavior, supervision, and any damage or injury caused by any dog or animal brought onto the Property by Renter or any Guest, and agrees to indemnify Owner for any resulting claim, injury, or damage, including claims by other Guests or third parties. Dogs must not be left unattended near the pool, hot tub, or cold plunge at any time.

7. Smoking

Smoking of any kind (including cigarettes, cigars, vaping, and cannabis) is prohibited inside the house at all times. Outdoors, smoking is allowed only away from the house, and cigarette butts must be fully extinguished and disposed of in a trash can, never on the ground. Violation may result in immediate removal with no refund, in addition to any cleaning or damage charges.

8. Assumption of Risk

Renter acknowledges that the Property is in a remote desert environment and includes amenities and conditions that carry inherent risks of injury, illness, or death. These include, without limitation:

  • A swimming pool and hot tub/jacuzzi, not supervised by lifeguards or staff at any time
  • A cold plunge and sauna, use of which carries cardiovascular and other health risks
  • An open fire pit and outdoor cooking/BBQ equipment
  • A disc golf course and associated equipment
  • Uneven, rocky, or unstable natural desert terrain, including on hiking trails on or near the Property
  • Wildlife, insects, and plants native to the desert, including but not limited to snakes, scorpions, and cacti
  • Extreme temperature swings, sun exposure, and limited cell phone reception
  • Low-light or unlit outdoor areas at night, including during stargazing or telescope use
  • The presence of dogs and other animals belonging to Renter’s party

Renter, on behalf of Renter and all Guests, voluntarily assumes full responsibility for any risk of loss, property damage, or personal injury, including death, arising from use of the Property or its amenities, whether or not caused by the negligence of Owner, except to the extent caused by Owner’s gross negligence, willful misconduct, or violation of law.

9. Care of Premises and Damages

Renter agrees to pay Owner for any damage to the house, furnishings, pool, or other Property amenities resulting from Renter’s or any Guest’s occupancy, including additional cleaning costs beyond the cleaning fee paid at booking, charged at Owner’s documented actual cost. Renter agrees to close and lock all doors and windows when the house is unoccupied, including at Check-Out.

10. Owner’s Rights

If the terms of this Agreement are violated, Owner may immediately cancel this Agreement and require Renter and all Guests to vacate the Property, by legal process or otherwise. Amounts paid by Renter may be forfeited as liquidated damages, and Owner may charge the payment method on file for additional documented damage or violation-related costs.

11. Release and Indemnification

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Renter, on behalf of Renter and all Guests, releases, waives, and discharges Owner, its members, managers, employees, and agents (“Released Parties”) from any liability, claim, or cause of action arising from any loss, damage, or injury sustained on the Property, excepting only the gross negligence or willful misconduct of the Released Parties. Renter agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Released Parties from any claim or expense (including reasonable attorneys’ fees) arising from Renter’s or any Guest’s use of the Property or breach of this Agreement.

12. No Subletting

Renter may not sublet, assign, or transfer this booking or any part of the Property to any other party without Owner’s prior written consent.

13. No Permanent Residence

Use of the Property under this Agreement is temporary, short-term, and transient only. The Property is not, and will not become, the permanent residence of Renter or any Guest.

14. Repairs and Maintenance

The Property is rented with the furnishings, fixtures, and equipment presently on site. Owner will make reasonable efforts to promptly address reported maintenance issues but cannot guarantee that all equipment or amenities (including the pool, hot tub, cold plunge, sauna, or disc golf equipment) will be in working order at all times. No rate adjustment or refund will be made for equipment or amenity outages.

15. No Daily Staff

The Property is a self-service, unstaffed rental. No daily housekeeping, front desk, or on-site security is provided during the reservation unless separately arranged in writing.

16. Minors

Renter represents that Renter is at least 18 years of age and has authority to enter this Agreement on behalf of any minor Guest. Renter acknowledges that a minor may retain independent legal rights under California law that cannot be waived by a parent or guardian, and this Agreement does not waive such rights held directly by the minor.

17. Security Camera

One exterior security camera is located at the front entrance and driveway and monitors vehicles and people arriving at the Property. There are no cameras covering the pool, hot tub, sauna, cold plunge, or any interior or private guest area.

18. Cancellations

Renter may cancel at any time. Owner will refund any refundable amount according to the Cancellation Policy at the top of this page, which applies at the time of booking.

19. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

20. Governing Law and Venue

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. Any dispute will be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in San Bernardino County, California.

Acknowledgment and Acceptance

By checking the acceptance box during online booking and completing payment, Renter certifies that Renter has read, understood, and voluntarily agrees to all terms of this Agreement on behalf of Renter and all Guests, and acknowledges receipt of the Property’s house rules.
