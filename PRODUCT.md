# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A broad mix of travelers drawn to Joshua Tree's desert magic: couples seeking romantic getaways, friend groups and event organizers planning retreats, solo travelers and spiritual seekers looking for something beyond ordinary glamping. Guests arrive wanting to disconnect from the everyday and reconnect with themselves, the desert, and something harder to name.

## Product Purpose

Alien Dog Campground is a glamping destination on 5 acres in Joshua Tree, California. It exists to deliver an experience that feels like stepping into another world — part desert retreat, part cosmic adventure, grounded in real hospitality. The campground is currently open and actively hosting guests. Bookings flow through Airbnb today, with a direct-booking website launching simultaneously to give guests a second channel.

## Positioning

Two things no competitor can copy: (1) the alien dog universe — four real rescue dogs, a family origin story that started with a dog named Ella and a t-shirt brand called The Alien Dog, and a visual identity built around that mythology; (2) the specific land — 5 acres of hand-built desert property with energy vortex spots, a sauna, jacuzzi, stargazing, and a spiritual dimension rooted in the place itself. The brand story and the land experience are equally irreplaceable.

## Operating Context

Guests book via Airbnb or (imminently) via the direct-booking website. The campground was hand-built by Carolina and Chris (a licensed General Contractor) along with Chris's father Don, over four years. Amenities include glamping accommodations, a cedar sauna, jacuzzi, outdoor kitchen, fire pit, stargazing area, and energy vortex sessions. Group and event inquiries are handled through a dedicated form on the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page static HTML/CSS/JS site (no framework, no build tooling)
- Booking calendar and group inquiry form are built into the page
- VIP email signup collects early-access interest
- Site embeds base64-encoded images (large file size as a result)
- Google Maps embed for location
- Instagram integration (links to @aliendogcampground)

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Alien Dog Campground
- Visual identity: cosmic/alien theme — neon green (#39ff14), purple (#9b5de5), dark space backgrounds
- Typography: Orbitron (display/headings), Exo 2 (body)
- Voice: mystical but grounded, playful but sincere — "we built a portal"
- The four dogs (cartoon + real photo reveals) are a signature brand element
- Additional brand assets (logo files, photography) exist on disk — path to be provided

## Evidence on Hand

- Real photography of the campground, dogs, and property (base64-embedded in current site and additional files on disk)
- Real origin story copy (Carolina, Chris, and Don building the property)
- Real amenity descriptions and guest experience details
- No fabricated testimonials, pricing claims, or metrics should be invented

## Product Principles

1. **The experience is the product.** Every design decision should make the visitor feel something — the site is the first moment of the Alien Dog universe, not a brochure about it.
2. **Real over invented.** Real dogs, real story, real place. Never fabricate social proof, guest counts, or claims not grounded in fact.
3. **Accessible magic.** The cosmic theme should enchant without excluding — the site must work for someone practically planning a trip as well as someone falling in love with the vibe.
4. **Two channels, one brand.** As direct booking launches alongside Airbnb, the website must stand on its own as a complete booking and discovery experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific standard was established. The current site uses high-contrast neon-on-dark colors; future work should maintain readable contrast ratios and keyboard navigability.
